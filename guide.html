GAME GUIDE

Every published system of Elysia Lunar, in one place. If a number is not on this page, it has not been decided yet — anyone quoting you other numbers is not speaking for the project.

1. The Lunar Cycle

The game runs in 28-day seasons called Lunar Cycles. Each cycle has four phases, and the whole world — events, wars, raids, rankings — is keyed to them.

PhaseDaysWhat opens
New Moon1–3World events, double gathering yields, cycle quest chain
Waxing4–14Coven wars — territory control on the Tidefall Coast
Full Moon15Colosseum of Tides finals; The Pale Wastes raid realm opens
Waning16–28Final arena grind; rankings lock at the end of day 27; Moonfall Reset on day 28

Season honours: the Top 10 Covens and the Top 300 players on the personal Colosseum ladder receive season titles and cosmetics, plus a share of the cycle's $SIA rewards allocation. The size of each cycle's allocation is announced before that cycle begins — it is never promised in advance, and rewards are paid only from an allocation that actually exists.

3. Lunar Covens (guilds)

  • Size: up to 40 members per Coven.
  • Facilities: Coven hall, shared vault, and a Coven banner crafted from moon-thread.
  • Coven wars: during the Waxing phase, Covens battle for territory on the Tidefall Coast. Holding territory grants gathering bonuses in the Golden Thread Fields — never direct token income.
  • Honours: Top 10 Covens each cycle receive season titles and cosmetics, plus a share of the cycle's announced $SIA rewards allocation.

4. Colosseum of Tides (arena)

  • Format: ranked 1v1 and 3v3 ladders, open all cycle; finals are held on Full Moon day (day 15) and rankings lock at the end of day 27.
  • Rewards: Moonshards per match (win or lose, with a daily cap), season honours for the Top 300.
  • Fair play: gear is normalised inside the Colosseum — Moon Relics give no stat advantage there. Skill and build decide the ladder.

5. Moonfall Reset rules

At the end of every cycle (day 28), the Moonfall Reset applies:

6. Economy

  • Moonshards — the in-game currency. Earned from the Colosseum, daily quests, and world events. Spent on crafting, repairs, and relic upgrades. The Thread Forge permanently burns shards used in high-tier crafting, and total shard issuance per cycle is published at cycle start and can only decrease from cycle to cycle.
  • $SIA — the project's token on Robinhood Chain. Fixed supply of 1,000,000,000, no mint function; 750,000,000 locked in a vesting vault. Used in-game for Moon Relic minting, cosmetics, and season rewards. The game never creates new $SIA.
  • No pay-to-win: everything purchasable is cosmetic or convenience. Colosseum gear is normalised.

7. Build phases

  • Phase 1 — Concept & website (current): world, systems, and economy published for community feedback.
  • Phase 2 — Playable browser beta: one realm (Mare of First Light), three classes, Colosseum ladder.
  • Phase 3 — Full launch: all five realms, Covens and wars, and Lunar Cycle 1 (Season 1).

No launch dates are promised. Each phase opens when it is ready and tested, and is announced only on the official X account, @ElysiaLunarYarn.
